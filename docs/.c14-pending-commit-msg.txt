*** A REAL BUG FOUND BY THE COVERAGE ANALYSIS, RECORDED BUT DELIBERATELY NOT
FIXED HERE. *** Settling _HandleMidJump's coverage turned up that
`HandleMidJump` (src/home/overworld.asm) has ZERO callers tree-wide, while pret
calls it from OverworldLoopLessDelay (home/overworld.asm:49) — `faithdiff
OverworldLoopLessDelay` has been reporting `DROPPED HandleMidJump (call)` all
along. HandleLedges IS reachable and sets BIT_LEDGE_OR_FISHING, and _HandleMidJump
is the only routine that clears it on the ledge path, so after one ledge hop the
flag sticks and permanently gates collision, OBJ and emotion bubbles. A `BUG{}`
annotation now sits on HandleMidJump (lints clean) and the detail, repro and fix
plan are in stigmergy memory `regression-overworld-ledge-hop-never-advanced`.
Not fixed here because restoring a call to the overworld main loop is a behaviour
change, and this commit's whole claim is that behaviour is unchanged; it is also
unverifiable today, since no scenario covers ledges. Scenario first, then the call.
